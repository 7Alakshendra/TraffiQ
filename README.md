TraffiQ is an AI-powered commute intelligence platform built specifically for Indian roads. Unlike conventional navigation apps, TraffiQ understands the ground realities of Indian traffic — waterlogging, cattle crossings, VVIP convoys, and wrong-side driving — by combining real-time computer vision on road footage with multilingual NLP on community reports and social media. The result is a live, hyperlocal risk map that helps daily commuters, drivers, and delivery agents make smarter travel decisions before they even step out the door.
